About Me

Computer Engineering student at Politecnico di Milano (Italy) with a technical background in Industrial Automation from COLTEC/UFMG. Experienced in integrating hardware and software, focusing on robotics, IoT, embedded programming (C/C++, Python), and 3D CAD modeling (SolidWorks, Fusion 360).

Currently Founder & IoT/Prototyping Developer at Vitrur, engineering integrated IoT and robotics solutions alongside additive manufacturing with high-performance filaments (such as Tritan HT). Formerly Junior Researcher & Prototyping Intern at LIPS (UFMG), developing functional healthcare IoT prototypes and industrial PLC automation logic in partnership with Petrobras.

Recognized with 1st Place at the CI-IA Health Challenge (Unimed-BH) for AI-applied hospital triage engineering, Academic Relevance Award at UFMG Knowledge Week for an elderly care wearable IoT device, and multiple awards in autonomous robotics at CoRA 2025 (1st Place Best Technical Design and 3rd Place Advanced Line Follower as team captain of Tartaruga Linhas).

Certified by institutions including Harvard University (TinyML, CS50 Web, CS50 Python), IBM (Computer Vision), OpenCV University, and IFMG (Industry 4.0 & IoT).
